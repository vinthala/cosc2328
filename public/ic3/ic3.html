Senor fish

This place is about Sr. Fish a small restaurant.. This restaurant serves everything from shrimp cockatails to whole bass plates, and includes children's dishes.

Location

Senior Fish is located in a border city of Texas, El Paso.

Why I love it

The reason I love this restaurant is not only because of the delicious menu items, but because of childhood memories. I have developed core memories with their food about family fun, and that could be you too.
